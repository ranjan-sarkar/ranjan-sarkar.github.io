Ranjan Sarkar

PhD Scholar, Artificial Intelligence

Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur

I am a PhD Research Scholar in the Department of Artificial Intelligence (DoAI) at IIT Kharagpur, advised by Dr. Prabhat Kumar Mishra. I work at the RHPI Lab (Receding Horizon & Predictive Intelligence Lab). My research focuses on the intersection of Reinforcement Learning, Estimation, and Optimization, with applications in LLMs, Computer Vision, and Robotics.
